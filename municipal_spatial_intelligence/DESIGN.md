## Brand & Style

The design system establishes an authoritative, reliable, and rigorous environment for municipal spatial planning, clean-energy grid alignment, and land-suitability intelligence. Rooted in institutional cartographic traditions and modern civil engineering workflows, the visual language prioritizes clarity, data legibility, and high-density spatial comprehension.

### Design Movement & Posture
The interface merges **Corporate Modernism** with **Cartographic Technical Utilitarianism**:
- **Structured Civic Authority:** Inspired by high-capacity geospatial infrastructures and municipal mapping portals, surfaces convey precision, institutional trust, and audit-readiness.
- **Cartographic Clarity:** Dark cybersecurity motifs, futuristic glow effects, heavy saturated gradients, and glassmorphic blurs are replaced by crisp, daylight-calibrated light surfaces, neutral slate division lines, and intentional whitespace.
- **Data-Dense Restraint:** Information is grouped within cleanly bounded cards and analytical tables, using restrained color accents exclusively for status verification, solar-energy viability, risk diagnostics, and cartographic feature identification.

## Colors

The color system transitions the interface into a daylight-readable municipal GIS environment. Chromatic hues are applied functionally to communicate geographic features, approval stages, and grid viability without visual noise.

### Role Allocations & Hierarchy
- **Primary (`#059669` Emerald):** Represents clean-energy approvals, high viability land parcels, recommended infrastructure sites, active navigation nodes, and primary civic action triggers.
- **Secondary (`#0284c7` Cobalt):** Used for hydrographic features, active cartographic geometry selection boundaries, measurement polyline tools, and spatial telemetry links.
- **Tertiary (`#d97706` Warm Amber):** Reserved for spatial planning review flags, moderate grid congestion notices, and provisional feasibility warnings.
- **Neutral (`#64748b` Slate Gray):** Establishes structural balance across secondary type, inactive coordinate badges, pending analysis states, and subtle spatial vectors.
- **Named Semantics:** Critical disqualifications and spatial exclusions utilize `#dc2626` (Crimson), counterbalanced by soft, tint-backed badges (`#fef2f2` / `#fecaca`) to maintain an official, composed report tone rather than an alarmist visual profile.

## Typography

Inter governs the entire typographic hierarchy, ensuring crisp line forms, legibility at minute scales, and balanced optical rendering across complex mapping sheets.

### Typographic Discipline
- **Tabular Alignment:** Activate `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` across all numerical readings, capacity calculators, geographical coordinates (lat/long), and dossier comparison tables to enforce strict vertical column tracking.
- **Case Formatting:** Header metrics, layer section dividers, and table columns must use `label-caps` in uppercase, styled in `#475569` or `#64748b` to distinctively frame values without heavy line dividers.
- **Hierarchy Separation:** Contrast between primary figures (`#0f172a`), contextual narrative notes (`#475569`), and structural metadata tags (`#94a3b8`) creates clear scanning pathways through high-density records.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built as an enterprise cartographic workstation, balancing full-bleed spatial map canvas interaction with fixed-width analytical modules.

### Structural Framework
- **Tri-Panel Desktop Operations Console:**
  - **Left Rail (320px–360px):** Layer hierarchy, environmental constraint toggles, zoning filters, and GIS baseline switchers.
  - **Central Workstation (Fluid):** Full-bleed interactive light cartographic viewport supporting vector tile overlays, parcel selection, and coordinate inspector pins.
  - **Right Inspector (380px–440px):** Site viability dossier, feasibility scoring engines, feeder distance matrices, and zoning compliance manifests.
- **Adaptive Adjustments:**
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Side panels collapse into dockable, slide-over drawer drawers (width: 360px) floating above the map.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Layer and dossier panels refactor into anchored bottom sheets, maximizing map interaction while providing quick swipe access to site validation status.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate surface separation, soft architectural shadows, and crisp neutral borders (`#e2e8f0`), strictly avoiding murky ambient casts or luminous outer glows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Basemap Plane):** `#f8fafc` — The neutral map viewport background housing cartographic land tiles, light waterway corridors (`#bae6fd`), and infrastructure paths.
- **Level 1 (Docked Layout Containers & App Header):** `#ffffff` — Grounded directly on the canvas, bounded by a 1px solid `#e2e8f0` stroke with no drop shadow.
- **Level 2 (Analytical Dossier Cards & Floating Tools):** `#ffffff` — Elevated above the basemap canvas using a refined composite shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` and framed with 1px `#e2e8f0`.
- **Level 3 (Hover Dossiers, Flyouts, & Select Menus):** `#ffffff` — Anchored floating menus use `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` bordered by `#cbd5e1`.
- **Level 4 (Audit Modals & Approval Overlays):** `#ffffff` centered against a light scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.40)` with zero backdrop blur), elevated by `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.10), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The interface implements a clean, structural geometry calibrated specifically for enterprise data density.

### Radius Specifications
- **Cards & Analytical Containers:** 8px to 10px corner radius (`rounded-lg`), providing an approachable modern feel while respecting dense tabular layouts.
- **Inputs, Buttons, & Tool Triggers:** 6px to 8px (`rounded-md` to `rounded-lg`) corner radius for sharp interaction targets.
- **Data Provenance Badges & Micro Chips:** 4px (`rounded-sm`) radius, preserving their role as technical, stamp-like indicators.
- **Map Control Floating Clusters (Zoom, Pan, Compass):** Grouped into segmented 8px rounded vertical pill assemblies with 1px internal dividers.

## Components

### Buttons & Primary Triggers
- **Primary Municipal Action (e.g., "Generate Feasibility Report"):** Solid `#059669` fill, `#ffffff` text, font-weight 600, 8px radius. Hover: `#047857`. Active: `#065f46`. Focus ring: 2px offset with `#059669`.
- **Secondary Geospatial Control (e.g., "Buffer Zone 1km", "Measure Corridor"):** `#ffffff` background, 1px border `#e2e8f0`, `#475569` text. Hover: `#f8fafc` fill with `#0f172a` text and `#cbd5e1` border.
- **Destructive / Exclude Parcel:** `#ffffff` fill, 1px border `#fecaca`, `#dc2626` text. Hover: `#fef2f2` fill, `#b91c1c` text.

### Status Indicators & Badges
- **Recommended / Optimal:** Background `#ecfdf5`, border 1px `#a7f3d0`, text `#059669`. Preceded by an emerald 6px solid dot.
- **Review Required / Moderate Risk:** Background `#fffbeb`, border 1px `#fde68a`, text `#d97706`. Preceded by an amber 6px solid dot.
- **High Risk / Exclusion:** Background `#fef2f2`, border 1px `#fecaca`, text `#dc2626`. Preceded by a crimson 6px solid dot.
- **Screening / Pending:** Background `#f8fafc`, border 1px `#e2e8f0`, text `#64748b`.

### Data Provenance Chips
- Micro chips (`tag-provenance`) attached to specific calculation blocks (`PROXY`, `DERIVED`, `ESTIMATED`, `VERIFIED`):
  - Height: 18px, uppercase, letter-spacing +0.05em, font-size 10px, 4px radius.
  - Neutral-cool aesthetic: `#f1f5f9` fill, `#cbd5e1` border, `#475569` text.

### Input Fields & Layer Controls
- **Form Fields & Dropdowns:** Pure `#ffffff` surface, 1px `#cbd5e1` border, `#0f172a` body text, `#94a3b8` placeholder. Height: 36px. Focused state: `#0284c7` border with a subtle 1px matching ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 16px square/circle, `#ffffff` background with 1px `#cbd5e1` border. Checked state: `#059669` fill with crisp white checkmark vector.
- **GIS Layer Items:** Horizontal stack containing a map symbology swatch (e.g., `#bae6fd` line for rivers, `#059669` fill for solar reserves), title in `body-sm` (`#0f172a`), trailing capacity metric in `label-caps` (`#64748b`), and a compact 14px visibility toggle.

### Analytical Dossier Cards
- Constructed with `#ffffff` fill, 1px `#e2e8f0` boundary, and 8px corners.
- **Card Header:** Subtly demarcated with `#f8fafc` background, 1px bottom border `#e2e8f0`, housing section titles in `title-md` and contextual provenance badges.
- **Card Body:** Generous internal whitespace (16px to 20px padding), displaying metrics using `metric-tabular` paired with descriptive metadata beneath in `#64748b`.
