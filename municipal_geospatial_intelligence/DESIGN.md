## Brand & Style

The visual narrative is engineered for municipal infrastructure decision-makers, urban energy planners, and utility grid operators. It projects sovereign authority, precision geospatial intelligence, and fiscal stewardship. The experience avoids flashy, sci-fi cyber aesthetics in favor of a mission-critical operations console: dense, unambiguous, low-fatigue, and deeply analytical.

### Visual Architecture
The design style integrates **Modern Technical Minimalism** with **Structured Tactical Paneling**:
- **Canvas Hierarchy:** High-efficiency dark theme utilizing deep slate-blacks that maximize viewport contrast for complex vector tile maps, heatmaps, and power feeder layers without causing ocular exhaustion during multi-hour analytical sessions.
- **Data Fidelity:** Clean-energy metrics and geospatial overlays command focus through targeted semantic hues. Information is compartmentalized in crisp, modular surface tiers bound by low-contrast technical borders.
- **Tone & Posture:** Institutional, utilitarian, resilient, and audit-ready. The system conveys reliability through rigorous alignment, micro-level data provenance indicators (Proxy, Derived, Estimated, Screened), and strict layout density.

## Colors

The palette operates under a high-fidelity geospatial and institutional data protocol. High-chroma accents are strictly functional and reserved for interactive states, decision scores, categorical overlays, and risk diagnostics.

### Color Tiers & Semantic Utility
- **Primary (`#10B981` Emerald / `#059669` Deep Emerald / `#34D399` Mint):** Signifies solar-EV feasibility, optimal viability scores, greenlit feeder capacity, high-confidence recommendations, and affirmative operational states.
- **Secondary (`#0EA5E9` Sky / `#0284C7` Cobalt):** Used for cartographic interactions, selection halos, parcel boundaries, geo-query tools, distance radii, and telemetry links.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` Amber):** Dedicated to municipal review queues, moderate grid congestion, transformer capacity warnings, and estimated baseline metrics.
- **Critical Alert (`#EF4444` Coral Red):** Reserved for spatial flood hazards, zoning conflicts, substational overdraws, and site disqualifications.
- **Neutral Foundation:** Built on a calibrated spectrum from `#0B0F17` (Deep Map Canvas) to `#111827` (App Base Shell), `#1A2234` (Primary Surface Pane), and `#1F293D` (Elevated Panel/Flyout), framed by `#2D3748` (Technical Border Slate).
- **Text & Data Contrast:** High-clarity `#F9FAFB` for primary metrics, `#9CA3AF` for analytical labels, and `#64748B` for secondary spatial metadata.

## Typography

Inter was selected across all analytical, display, and label roles to maximize structural legibility, tabular integrity, and micro-scale rendering in information-dense dashboards.

### Editorial Guidelines
- **Tabular Figures:** Always apply OpenType tabular numerical settings (`tnum`, `cv05`, `cv08`) to grid metrics, coordinate panels, and capacity indicators to guarantee column vertical tracking.
- **Provenance Badges (`tag-provenance`):** Applied strictly to data-origin chips (`PROXY`, `DERIVED`, `ESTIMATED`, `SCREENED`). Must always be rendered in uppercase with positive tracking (+0.06em).
- **Labeling Hierarchy:** Metric descriptors and axis tags use `label-caps` in neutral muted tones (`#9CA3AF`) to maintain distinct separation from primary live readings.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform is designed around a spatial-first, viewport-constrained operations desk layout.

### Layout Model
- **Cartographic Viewport:** The default desktop shell pins a full-bleed interactive map canvas on the base plane, with dockable, collapsible analytical sidebars and metric panels overhanging the viewport.
- **Fixed/Fluid Hybrid Panel Grid:** Lateral toolbars and attribute tables lock to fixed, calibrated widths (360px–420px desktop width) to keep geographic interaction stable, while internal content flows flexibly with standard 8px/12px component intervals.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** Tri-pane layout (Left: Layer and scenario engine; Center: Geospatial viewport; Right: Viability dossier and power distribution sheet).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Collapsible drawer architecture; map remains primary with floating drawer tabs.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Bottom sheet modal architecture; metrics collapse into swipeable cards anchored over the map viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids soft, atmospheric drop-shadows that distract from geospatial vector layers. Depth is established through **Tonal Layer Stacking** paired with **Micro-Engineered Ghost Outlines**.

### Elevation Stack
- **Level 0 (Map Base / Canvas):** `#0B0F17` – True bottom layer containing satellite imagery, parcel shapes, and street grids.
- **Level 1 (Docked Structural Sidebars):** `#111827` – Low-reflection structural surface backed by a 1px border of `#2D3748`.
- **Level 2 (Analytical Cards, Sub-panels, Inspectors):** `#1A2234` – Elevated interactive panels, segmented by internal 1px `#2D3748` boundaries with a subtle ambient occlusion drop shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts, Hover Dossiers, Contextual Popovers):** `#1F293D` – Dynamic contextual inspection panels, elevated with a crisp border accent: `0 12px 28px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`, rimmed with 1px `#3E4C63`.
- **Level 4 (Modal Approvals & Critical Warnings):** `#111827` overlaid on `#0B0F17` at 75% opacity with 4px backdrop blur, surrounded by an alert-specific illuminated 1px perimeter.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a strict, architectural **Soft/Compact (`1`)** shape geometry. 

- Standard inputs, buttons, tabular cells, and floating chips use **4px (`0.25rem`)** corners.
- Contextual modal dialogs and docked slide-out panels use **8px (`0.5rem`)** corners.
- Provenance tags and technical data chips retain an engineered **2px to 4px** radius to emphasize their structured, non-consumer character.
- Radial map controllers and zoom toggles use sharp-cornered square modules grouped into unified 4px bounding containers.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary Municipal Action (e.g., "Export Feasibility Study", "Commit Site Scenario"):** Solid `#10B981` background, `#0B0F17` text, font-weight 600, 4px corner radius. Hover: `#34D399`. Active: `#059669`.
- **Geospatial / Secondary Tool (e.g., "Buffer Radius 500m", "Trace Feeder"):** `#1A2234` background, 1px border in `#2D3748`, `#F9FAFB` text. Hover: Border shifts to `#0EA5E9`, text to `#38BDF8`.
- **Destructive / Disqualify Site:** `#1A2234` background, 1px `#EF4444` border, `#EF4444` text. Hover: Solid `#EF4444` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Data Provenance & Metric Chips
Compact indicators that indicate data credibility and generation methodology:
- **Proxy:** Dark plum tint (`rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.15)`), 1px border `#A855F7`, `#C084FC` text.
- **Derived:** Indigo tint (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.15)`), 1px border `#6366F1`, `#818CF8` text.
- **Estimated:** Amber tint (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`), 1px border `#F59E0B`, `#FBBF24` text.
- **Screened:** Mint tint (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`), 1px border `#10B981`, `#34D399` text.
- *Layout:* Inline-flex, 2px corner radius, padding 2px 6px, tracking 0.06em, font size 10px bold.

### Form Inputs & Geo-Filters
- **Input Field & Select:** Dark slate surface `#111827`, continuous border 1px `#2D3748`, `#F9FAFB` text, `#64748B` placeholder. Focused: 1px `#0EA5E9` ring, zero blur.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 14px square/circle, `#1A2234` fill with 1px `#2D3748`. Checked state: `#10B981` solid with white micro checkmark icon.

### Data Grid & Spatial Layer Lists
- **Table Headers:** `#111827` surface, uppercase `label-caps` typography, 1px border-bottom `#2D3748`, zero vertical padding inflation (32px row height).
- **Row Styling:** `#1A2234` alternating with `#161D2B`, hover highlight in `#1F293D`. Left margin indicates viability via a 3px accent ribbon (`#10B981`, `#F59E0B`, or `#EF4444`).
- **Layer Toggle Items:** Dual state control with eye visibility icon, capacity tag, opacity slider integrated inline, and active color swatch matching the vector map layer.

### Analytical Dossier Cards
- Constructed with `#1A2234` surface, 1px continuous border `#2D3748`, divided into a 32px header strip (`#111827`) housing metric titles and provenance chips, followed by a padded metric body with tabular numerical values (`metric-tabular`).
